Verify the software independently

Place the three downloaded files in the same directory. Then run:

gpg --import mypubkey-new.asc
gpg --fingerprint 29B7AA73720168E2E21A546B5DE6353FAA731507
gpg --verify httpd.bz2.gpg httpd.bz2

GnuPG should report a Good signature issued by Angel Alfredo. Compare the full primary fingerprint to 29B7AA73720168E2E21A546B5DE6353FAA731507.

Expected SHA-256 checksum for httpd.bz2:

Check on Linux/macOS with sha256sum httpd.bz2 or on Windows PowerShell with Get-FileHash .\httpd.bz2 -Algorithm SHA256.
